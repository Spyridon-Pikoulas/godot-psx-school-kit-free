# PSX School Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build an abandoned classroom** for your survival horror game: vinyl tile, a ceiling of tiles and a light panel, classroom walls whole and scribbled on in crayon, the classroom door in its doorway, a steel window whose top light tips open, a chalkboard written over (I WILL NOT LEAVE), pupils' desks whose lids lift, their chairs, a clock that runs backwards and a child's handprints. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX School Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-school-kit)** has 136 pieces and 20 animations
for a whole school wing: corridors lined with lockers, classrooms wrecked and barricaded, a
two-storey gym with its bleachers and climbing rope, the toilets, the office, a stairwell up to a
chained floor, 29 seamless textures, a Unity package and a demo level you can walk through in your
browser. Same addon, same look: it installs over this one. It shares
[PSX Hospital Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-hospital-kit)'s and
[PSX Apartment Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-apartment-kit)'s grid, and their samplers,
[PSX Hospital Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-hospital-kit-free) and
[PSX Apartment Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-apartment-kit-free), are free too.

### What's inside

- **Structure:** a vinyl tile floor, a tiled ceiling, a classroom wall, one scribbled on in
  crayon, a window wall, a classroom doorway and a corner post, on a 2 m grid, 3 m high.
- **The classroom door** that swings open, its colliders moving with it, and **a steel window**
  whose top light tips open.
- **The classroom:** a written chalkboard, a pupil's desk whose lid lifts, its chair, a clock whose
  hand runs backwards, a ceiling light panel.
- **Mess:** a child's handprints, low on the wall.
- **4 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor, wall and ceiling
  textures as their own PNGs.
- 8 to 196 triangles a piece, one 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (a doorway's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your flashlight falls on it, and
a gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# door: the piece in your level
door.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

For the sound of it, [PSX Horror SFX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx) has footsteps
on concrete, tile and metal, creaking doors, creatures, stingers and the typewriter save,
PS1-style ([30 free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx-free)).
